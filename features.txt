My proposed roadmap:

  1. Playback queue

  - “Play next” and “Add to queue”
  - Reorder and remove queued songs
  - Save the current queue as a playlist
  - Persist the queue after closing the app

  2. Better listen-once experience

  - Start playback as soon as enough audio is available instead of waiting for the entire download
  - Show buffering/progress clearly
  - Automatically play the next queued listen-once song
  - Configurable cache size with “Clear temporary music”

  3. Playlist improvements

  - Rename playlists
  - Reorder songs
  - Playlist artwork based on the first four songs
  - Download an entire playlist
  - Export/import playlists
  - Display whether a playlist song is downloaded or online-only

  4. Library quality

  - Sort by title, artist, recently added, duration, and file size
  - Filter by artist or album
  - Multi-select songs for deletion or playlist assignment
  - Detect and clean duplicates
  - Show total storage used
  - Restore missing metadata and artwork

  5. Player improvements

  - Shuffle and repeat-all alongside repeat-one
  - Sleep timer
  - Playback speed
  - Volume normalization
  - Audio fade between tracks
  - Swipe artwork to change tracks
  - Lyrics support using user-provided local lyric files

  6. Search improvements

  - Search history and recent searches
  - Artist, album, playlist, and song result categories
  - Search suggestions
  - Better “official release” filtering
  - Open an artist to browse their songs and albums

  7. Reliability

  - Resume interrupted downloads
  - Download queue supporting multiple songs
  - Clear explanations for unavailable or region-restricted songs
  - Wi-Fi-only download setting
  - Storage-space warnings before large playlist downloads

  8. Backup and portability

  - Playlist backup is now implemented in Music/Pattalu
  - Add manual export/import controls
  - Back up listening preferences and queue
  - Provide a backup-health indicator
  - Support importing ordinary M3U playlists

  9. Android integration

  - Media notification actions
  - Lock-screen artwork and controls
  - Bluetooth/headset controls
  - Android Auto support
  - Home-screen player widget
  - Quick Settings play/pause tile

  10. Privacy and polish

  - No account required
  - No tracking by default
  - Material You adaptive colors
  - Proper empty, loading, and offline states
  - Accessibility labels and large-touch-target mode
  - Tamil and other language localization

  My highest-value next release would contain:

  - Persistent/reorderable playback queue
  - Shuffle and repeat-all
  - Sleep timer
  - Playlist rename and song reordering
  - Multi-song download queue
  - Storage/cache management
  - Improved artist and album browsing

  These improve everyday listening without introducing unreliable recommendation logic or unnecessary account infrastructure.
